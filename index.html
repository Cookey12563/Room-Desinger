📖 HOW TO USE

1

What Is a Room Box?

A Room box represents the four walls of a real room, like your bedroom or kitchen. Think of it as looking at the room from above, like a map. Click + Room in the top bar to add one.

2

What Is a Furniture Box?

A Furniture box is any object in the room — a bed, a desk, a couch, a table, etc. Drag these inside your room box to plan out where everything goes. Click + Furniture to add one.

3

Moving Things Around

Click any box to select it. Then click and drag it to move it around the canvas. Everything snaps to the grid automatically, so things stay neatly aligned.

4

Making a Box Bigger or Smaller

When a box is selected, four small white squares appear at the corners. Drag any corner square to resize the box. The size shown inside updates live so you always know the real-world measurement.

5

Renaming a Box

Double-click the label text inside any box. A text field appears — type your new name, then press Enter or click somewhere else to save it. Great for labeling things like "My Bed" or "Bookshelf."

6

Checking the Measurements

Hover your mouse over any box to see a popup showing the size in every unit: pixels, centimeters, meters, inches, feet, and feet+inches. The app scale is 1 grid cell = 10 cm, so you can use real room sizes.

7

Deleting a Box

Select a box and a small ✕ button appears in the top-right corner of it. Click it to delete that one box. To wipe everything at once, use the ✕ Clear button in the toolbar at the top. You can also press Delete on your keyboard after selecting a box.

💡 Pro tip: Start by adding a Room box and resizing it to match your actual room. Then add Furniture boxes inside it and resize each one to match your real furniture dimensions. Hover any box to double-check the measurements match up in the units you prefer!
